# Feature & Business Value Mapping

This document maps the **114+ API Endpoints** in this repository to their specific business value and user outcomes.

## 1. Professional Onboarding & Personalization

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/onboarding/*`, `/api/v1/assessments/diagnostic/*`

-   **User Outcome**: Learners don't waste time on things they already know.
-   **Business Value**: Faster "Time to Value" for the learner, higher completion rates.

## 2. AI-Driven Adaptive Learning

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/ai/*`, `/api/v1/learning/path/*`

-   **User Outcome**: 24/7 personalized coaching that feels like a private human tutor.
-   **Business Value**: Reduced cost of support, proprietary AI dataset growth, and 10x scalability compared to traditional LMS.

## 3. Enterprise Training Hub

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/enterprise/*`

-   **User Outcome**: Companies can manage thousands of employees across teams with custom branding.
-   **Business Value**: High-ticket B2B sales potential, administrative efficiency for HR departments.

## 4. Quality Assurance & Tutor Workflow

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/cms/*`, `/api/v1/inbox/*`, `/api/v1/admin/content/*`

-   **User Outcome**: Content is accurate, reviewed, and human tutors are available exactly when needed.
-   **Business Value**: Brand protection through human-in-the-loop validation and scalable tutor payouts.

## 5. Certification & Career Growth

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/certificates/*`, `/api/v1/assessments/final/*`

-   **User Outcome**: Provable, shareable skills for career advancement.
-   **Business Value**: Viral marketing through LinkedIn sharing, learner retention, and industry credibility.

## 6. Compliance & Data Integrity

**Endpoints**: `/api/v1/admin/ndpr/*`, `/api/v1/admin/audit/*`

-   **User Outcome**: Data is handled securely and can be deleted/exported on request.
-   **Business Value**: Full legal compliance (NDPR/GDPR), minimizing legal risk and building user trust.

---

**Summary**: The backend is ready to support an Alpha launch with core loops for individual learners (B2C) and initial pilot programs for Corporate organizations (B2B).
